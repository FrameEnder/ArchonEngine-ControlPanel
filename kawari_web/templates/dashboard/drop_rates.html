Drop rates

How often and how much items drop, and how much gil and EXP is given. Every rate is a percent of the server's own: 100 is as it is (retail's, where that's known), 200 twice as often or twice as much, 0 never, up to {{ most }}. A box left blank takes what's above it, shown greyed in the box. The one exception is Mounts & minions, which is the chance itself (0 to 100; {{ mounts_default }} to start with).

The nearest setting wins. For an item dropping somewhere, the server looks at that duty's or zone's own row, then its kind of content (or Open world), then its expansion, then Everywhere; in the first of those that says anything for the item it takes the item's category, else its group, else that row's All items.
