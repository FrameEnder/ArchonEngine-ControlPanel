Battle replay

A retail battle and a Kawari one on the same top-down map, on one clock: where everyone stood and faced, each cast's area as the client draws it, who hit whom for how much, knockbacks, deaths, and your !marks. The retail side is a battle run (Kawari Recorder's, or one made from a Chronofoil capture with questkit chronofoil --battle N.V --out run.json.gz, uploaded below); the Kawari side is one zone visit of a packet capture (Diagnostics → Packet capture). Either side can be either, to compare two retail runs or two tries on Kawari.
